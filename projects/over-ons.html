About Ashwini

wWe have been committed to empowering marginalized communities through health and education.

[Image Placeholder 1]

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive, accessible, and compassionate healthcare and educational opportunities to the most vulnerable populations in rural areas. We focus on long-term sustainable development and community independence.

Our Vision

A world where every individual, regardless of their social or economic background, has access to basic human rights, especially quality healthcare and education, enabling them to lead healthy and fulfilling lives.

Our History

Founded in 1989 by a group of passionate doctors, Ashwini started as a small mobile clinic addressing urgent health needs in remote villages. Our growth has been fueled by the unwavering support of local communities and international partners.
